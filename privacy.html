Nanova Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Nanova ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting student privacy and handling personal information with the highest institutional standards.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to deliver offline academic practice and track study progress:

  • Phone number (used solely as an institutional identifier for student account authentication).
  • Academic profile preferences (selected university, academic stream, and student display name).
  • Study performance telemetry (answers submitted, bookmarks, and simulated exam scores cached locally on device).

2. Purpose of Processing

Collected information is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate students and maintain curriculum access state across sessions.
  • Synchronize practice questions and official university campus announcements.
  • Enable offline IndexedDB caching for zero-data exam preparation in regions with limited connectivity.

3. Third-Party Infrastructure

Nanova utilizes Google Firebase Authentication and Firebase Realtime Database for encrypted profile data storage and authentication. We do not sell, lease, or monetize student personal information to any third parties or advertising networks.

4. User-Generated Content & Community Safety

The Community Feed facilitates official announcements and student peer comments. All users have access to real-time "Report Post" and "Block User" controls to report objectionable material and immediately block disruptive authors. Reported content is audited by moderators within 24 hours.

5. Offline Data Storage

Academic exam questions and user progress are stored client-side in the browser's IndexedDB and localStorage. Students may clear local data at any time via the "Reset Offline Data" option in the Profile tab.

6. Children's Privacy

Nanova is targeted towards university and higher education students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

7. Security Safeguards

All transmission of student data is encrypted using standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Authentication records are secured through Firebase Authentication.

8

Section 8: Student Account & Data Deletion Requests

In compliance with user data privacy standards and policies on Account Deletion, students have the full right to permanently delete their account and associated data stored in Nanova servers.

What data will be permanently deleted:

  • Authentication credentials (phone number and user ID).
  • User profile records (name, university, stream, and access status).
  • Academic token submission records and history.
  • Any comments or likes published on the community feed.

How to request data deletion:

You can request immediate deletion through either of the following methods:

  1. In-App Deletion: Go to the Profile tab, select Reset Offline Data, and click Sign Out to purge all local device records.
  2. Direct Server Deletion Request: Send an email to our academic data privacy desk at privacy@nanova.et with your registered phone number. Account records and associated cloud database entries will be permanently expunged within 48 hours.

No account data is retained after deletion confirmation has been processed.
